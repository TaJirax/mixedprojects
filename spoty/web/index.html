Instagram

Paste a Reel, post, or story-highlight link, or type @username for a whole profile. Photos and video both come down at source quality.

posts

Track · set · profile

SoundCloud

Paste a track, playlist, or profile link. Audio uses the format and bitrate selected in Download settings.

tracks

SoundCloud Go/Go+ members can sign in to use streams their account is entitled to. The app never bypasses DRM; a track that remains DRM-encrypted cannot be downloaded.

Video · photo · GIF · Space

X

Paste any x.com or twitter.com post. Video comes down at the quality you pick; photo posts, GIFs and mixed posts are collected at full resolution.

Video is fetched with yt-dlp. When a post carries no video — a photo, a GIF, a multi-image post — the app falls through to gallery-dl automatically, so every media type on X lands the same way. Public posts usually work without signing in; use sign-in for followers-only or age-gated posts.

Supported sites · direct media

Video

Paste a public video page or direct media link. yt-dlp handles regular video, Streamlink covers live streams, and HTML5, gallery, and direct-file fallbacks cover the rest. YouTube links stay on the dedicated YouTube page.

items

Only download media you have the right to save. DRM-protected, paywalled, and login-only media are not bypassed.

PDF · EPUB · MOBI · AZW3 · FB2

Documents & Ebooks

Paste a direct document or ebook link, or a page that links to files. Valid downloads keep their original format.

links checked

Files are checked from their contents, not just their names. Common documents, Office files, ebooks, comic archives, and text formats are supported.

Sites that build their viewer in JavaScript can hide the real file. Open the document itself and paste that address when an index page finds nothing.
